Grammar Rules: Direct vs. Indirect Questions

Indirect questions use statement word order and start with polite phrases like "Could you explain..." or "I would like to know...".

Direct Question (Question order)

"Why did you choose this field?"

Indirect Question (Statement order)

"Could you explain why you chose this field?"
